Athlete Performance

High Performance Training for School Athletes

As a former high school wrestler and football player, I understand firsthand how much sports can shape an athlete beyond strength and conditioning. The discipline, confidence, resilience, and ability to respond to coaching all play a major role in how young athletes develop, both in competition and outside of it.

Today, I coach Developmental Tennis at Sacred Heart Greenwich, where I help introduce young athletes to the sport while developing the habits, confidence, and competitive mindset that help them grow. During the spring season, I also coach the fifth- and sixth-grade tennis team, giving me the opportunity to work with young athletes at an important stage of their development.

That experience shapes the way I approach High Performance training at PCTC. Sessions are capped at 4 athletes so each athlete receives real coaching, individual movement corrections, and attention to their specific needs. We work on the physical qualities that carry over to almost every sport, including first-step quickness, acceleration, agility, change of direction, balance, coordination, strength, power, and conditioning.

No matter what sport an athlete plays, the goal is to improve overall athletic performance. I want athletes to move more efficiently, react faster, become stronger and more explosive, and build the confidence to compete at a higher level. The goal is not simply to make athletes tired. It is to help them become better athletes.

As a former high school wrestler and football player, and now a developmental tennis coach at Sacred Heart Greenwich, I understand both the physical and mental side of developing young athletes.

Sessions are capped at 4 athletes and focus on first-step quickness, acceleration, agility, change of direction, coordination, strength, power, and conditioning. No matter the sport, the goal is better overall athletic performance.

Sessions: Monday–Thursday | 4:30 PM–5:25 PM Investment: $60 per athlete

Before You Start

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get started?

Book a free intro class or choose a membership that fits your schedule.

What is the difference between Perform and Sweat?

Perform focuses on strength, power, athletic movement, and functional training. Sweat is a 45-minute conditioning class combining cardio, core, free weights, bodyweight movements, and functional circuits.

What is the founding membership?

The founding membership is limited to the first 25 members and includes unlimited classes for $150 per month for the first year, then converts to the regular Unlimited membership at $175 per month. It is month-to-month, not a one-year commitment. The $5 reservation payment is credited toward your first month. PushPress before signing up.

How do membership cancellations work?

Memberships are month-to-month. Submit your cancellation before your next billing date to stop the next renewal charge. Email contact@pctrainingclub.com or contact us using the details below. This timing does not limit cancellation or refund rights provided by law.

Can I take a class without a membership?

Yes. A $25 drop-in includes one Sweat or Perform class.

Do you offer personal training?

Yes. PCTC offers one-on-one personal training and semi-private training. Contact us to discuss your goals and availability.

How does Athlete Performance work?

Sessions for school athletes are capped at four athletes and focus on speed, agility, strength, power, coordination, and conditioning. Sessions run Monday–Thursday, 4:30–5:25 PM, at $60 per athlete. See Athlete Performance and reserve a spot.
